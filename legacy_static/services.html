WHAT WE OFFER

Our Healthcare Services

Professional home medical care by certified staff — available 24/7 at your doorstep in Shakargarh & Narowal.

Injection Service

Safe and hygienic injection administration at your home. Our trained professionals handle all types of injections including Intramuscular (IM), Intravenous (IV), and Subcutaneous (SC) — including insulin and antibiotic injections. We use disposable syringes and follow strict safety protocols.

  • Intramuscular (IM) Injections
  • Intravenous (IV) Injections
  • Subcutaneous (SC) Injections
  • Insulin Administration
  • Disposable Syringes Only

Wound Dressing

Expert wound care and dressing services at home. We handle post-surgical wounds, diabetic ulcers, burns, pressure sores, and general injuries. Our staff uses sterile medical-grade supplies and follows proper infection control procedures for safe healing.

  • Post-Surgical Wound Care
  • Diabetic Wound Management
  • Burn Dressing & Care
  • Pressure Sore Treatment
  • Sterile Medical Supplies

IV Drip Therapy

Professional intravenous drip therapy administered at your home under careful supervision. We provide hydration therapy, antibiotic drips, vitamin infusions, and medication drips as prescribed by your doctor. Continuous monitoring throughout the procedure.

  • IV Fluid & Hydration Therapy
  • Antibiotic IV Drips
  • Medication Infusions
  • Vitamin Drip Therapy
  • Continuous Patient Monitoring

Blood Sampling

Convenient home blood collection for laboratory tests. No need to visit a lab — our trained phlebotomist comes to your door. We collect samples for CBC, blood sugar, thyroid, liver function, lipid profile, and all other routine and specialized lab tests.

  • CBC & Blood Sugar Tests
  • Thyroid & Liver Function
  • Lipid Profile & Cholesterol
  • Safe Sample Collection
  • Quick & Accurate Results

Catheter Care

Professional urinary catheter insertion, removal, and maintenance at home. Our trained nursing staff ensures hygienic catheter management for bedridden patients, elderly individuals, and post-surgical recovery. Includes routine catheter changes and infection prevention care.

  • Catheter Insertion & Removal
  • Routine Catheter Changes
  • Infection Prevention Care
  • Bedridden Patient Support
  • Elderly & Post-Op Care

Round-the-clock emergency medical response at your doorstep. When you need urgent medical help — day or night — our emergency team is ready. We provide first aid, stabilization, emergency injections, IV drips, and vital signs monitoring until the situation is under control.
